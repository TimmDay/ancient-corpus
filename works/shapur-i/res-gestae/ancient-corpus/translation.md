# Res Gestae Divi Saporis: our English translation

Translated from Huyse's transcription of the Parthian version (1999), one section per chapter, with his chapter numbers. Made with Claude Opus 5.5 on 30 October 2026 (docs/adr/0003). Square brackets mark words restored in the damaged inscription or added for sense; "(?)" marks a doubtful reading.

## 1

I, the Mazda-worshipping lord Shapur, king of kings of Iran and non-Iran, whose lineage is from the gods, son of the Mazda-worshipping lord Ardashir, king of kings of Iran, whose lineage is from the gods, grandson of the lord King Pabag, am ruler of the Empire of Iran.

## 2

And I hold the lands of Persis, Parthia, Khuzestan, Mesene, Asurestan, Nodshiragan, Arabia, Adurbadagan, Armenia, Iberia, Sigan, Albania, Balasagan, as far as the Caucasus mountains and the Gate of the Alans, and the whole of the Elburz range,

## 3

Media, Gurgan, Merv, Herat and all of Abarshahr, Kerman, Sakastan, Turan, Makran, Paradan, India, and the Kushan Empire as far as Peshawar (?) and as far as Kashgar, Sogdiana and Chach, and on the far side of the sea the land of Mazun.

## 4

And I named [one city] Peroz-Shapur, the … land (?), and named [another] Ohrmazd-Ardashir (written above: Shapur).

## 5

And all these lands, rulers and governors became tributary and subject to us.

## 6

And when I had first become established over the land, Gordian Caesar gathered a force from all of Rome, Gothia and the lands of Germany, and came to Asurestan, against the Empire of Iran and against us. And on the border of Asurestan, at Misiche, there was a great battle face to face.

## 7

Gordian Caesar was killed and the Roman force was destroyed (?), and the Romans made Philip Caesar.

## 8

And Philip Caesar came to us to beg, and gave us 500,000 denarii as the price of their lives (?), and became tributary. And because of this we named Misiche Peroz-Shapur.

## 9

And Caesar lied again and did wrong to Armenia. And we marched on the land of the Romans, and destroyed a Roman force of 60,000 at Barbalissos.

## 10

And the land of Syria, and what lay around the land of Syria, we burned, ruined and plundered all of it. And in that one campaign we took from the land of the Romans these fortresses and cities:

## 11

the city of Anatha with its surroundings, Birtha of Arupan with its surroundings, Birtha of Asporakan with its surroundings, the city of Sura with its surroundings, the city of Barbalissos with its surroundings, the city of Hierapolis with its surroundings,

## 12

the city of Beroea with its surroundings, the city of Chalcis with its surroundings, the city of Apamea with its surroundings, the city of Raphanea with its surroundings, the city of Zeugma with its surroundings, the city of Urima with its surroundings,

## 13

the city of Gindaros with its surroundings, the city of Larmenaz with its surroundings, the city of Seleucia with its surroundings, the city of Antioch with its surroundings, the city of Cyrrhus with its surroundings,

## 14

the other city of Seleucia with its surroundings, the city of Alexandria with its surroundings, the city of Nicopolis with its surroundings, the city of Sinzara with its surroundings, the city of Hamath with its surroundings,

## 15

the city of Ariston with its surroundings, the city of Dichor with its surroundings, the city of Dura with its surroundings, the city of Doliche with its surroundings, the city of Circesium with its surroundings, the city of Germanicia with its surroundings,

## 16

the city of Batnae with its surroundings, the city of Khanar with its surroundings; in Cappadocia, the city of Satala with its surroundings, the city of Domana with its surroundings,

## 17

the city of Artangil with its surroundings, the city of Suisa with its surroundings, the city of Sinda with its surroundings, the city of Phreata with its surroundings: in all, 37 cities with their surroundings.

## 18

In the third campaign, when we marched on Carrhae and Edessa and were besieging Carrhae and Edessa, Valerian Caesar came against us,

## 19

and there was with him [a force] from the land of Germany, from Raetia, from Noricum, from Dacia, from Pannonia, from Moesia, from Istria (?), from Spain, from Africa,

## 20

from Thrace, from Bithynia, from Asia, from Pamphylia, from Isauria, from Lycaonia, from Galatia, from Lycia, from Cilicia, from Cappadocia, from Phrygia, from Syria,

## 21

from Phoenicia, from Judaea, from Arabia, from Mauretania, from Germany, from Rhodes, from … (?), from Mesopotamia: a force of 70,000.

## 22

And on the far side of Carrhae and Edessa there was a great battle with Valerian Caesar, and we took Valerian Caesar prisoner with our own hands, and the rest, the praetorian prefect (?), the senators and the officers who were leaders of that force, we took them all prisoner and carried them off to Persis.

## 23

And the land of Syria, the land of Cilicia and the land of Cappadocia we burned, ruined and plundered.

## 24

And in that campaign we took from the land of the Romans the city of Alexandria by Issus with its surroundings, the city of Samosata with its surroundings, the city of Katabolos with its surroundings, the city of Aegeae with its surroundings, the city of Mopsuestia with its surroundings,

## 25

the city of Mallos with its surroundings, the city of Adana with its surroundings, the city of Tarsus with its surroundings, the city of Zephyrion with its surroundings, the city of Sebaste with its surroundings, the city of Corycus with its surroundings,

## 26

the city of Anazarbos with its surroundings, the city of Castabala with its surroundings, the city of Neronias with its surroundings, the city of Flavias with its surroundings, the city of Nicopolis with its surroundings,

## 27

the city of Epiphania with its surroundings, the city of Kelenderis with its surroundings, the city of Anemurium with its surroundings, the city of Selinus with its surroundings, the city of Myonpolis with its surroundings, the city of Antioch with its surroundings, the city of Seleucia (?) with its surroundings,

## 28

the city of Domitiopolis with its surroundings, the city of Tyana with its surroundings, the city of Caesarea with its surroundings, the city of Comana with its surroundings, the city of Kybistra with its surroundings,

## 29

the city of Sebasteia with its surroundings, the city of Birtha with its surroundings, the city of Rakoundia with its surroundings, the city of Laranda with its surroundings, the city of Iconium with its surroundings: in all, 36 cities with their surroundings.

## 30

And the people whom we carried off from the land of the Romans, from non-Iran, as plunder, we settled in the Empire of Iran, in Persis, Parthia, Khuzestan, Asurestan and the other lands, land by land, where there were estates of ours, of our father, of our grandfathers and of our forefathers.

## 31

And we sought out many other lands besides, and won much fame and bravery, which we have not written here; but we ordered this much to be written so that whoever comes after us may know this fame, bravery and rule of ours.

## 32

And for this reason, because the gods have made us their own in this way, and with the gods' support we have sought out and hold all these lands, we too have founded many Wahram fires land by land, and have done many good works for the magi, and have greatly increased the worship of the gods.

## 33

And these too we set down in writing: one fire named Husraw-Shapur, for our own soul and fame; one fire named Husraw-Adur-Anahid, for the soul and fame of our daughter Adur-Anahid, queen of queens; one fire named Husraw-Ohrmazd-Ardashir, for the soul and fame of our son Ohrmazd-Ardashir, great king of Armenia;

## 34

another fire named Husraw-Shapur, for the soul and fame of our son Shapur, king of Mesene; one fire named Husraw-Narseh, for the soul and fame of our son, the Aryan, Mazda-worshipping Narseh, king of India, Sakastan and Turan as far as the shore of the sea.

## 35

And what we give to these fires, and what was established (?), we have written all of it on a charter. And of the 1,000 lambs that came to us as surplus (?), and which we gave to these fires, we order that it be done according to custom:

## 36

for our soul, day by day, one lamb, one *griw* of bread and 5 *hofan*, [and] 4 *pas* of wine; for Lord Sasan, King Pabag, King Shapur son of Pabag, Ardashir king of kings, Queen Khwaranzem of the realm, Adur-Anahid queen of queens, Queen Denag, Wahram king of Gilan, Shapur king of Mesene,

## 37

Ohrmazd-Ardashir great king of Armenia, Narseh king of the Sakas, Shapurdukhtag queen of the Sakas, Lady Chashmag, Prince Peroz, Lady Murrod (?), mother of Shapur king of kings, Prince Narseh, Princess Roddukht daughter of Anoshag, Warazdukht daughter of Khwaranzem,

## 38

Queen Stakhryad, and Hormezdag son of the king of Armenia, Hormezd and Hormezdag Odabakht and Wahram, Shapur, Peroz, sons of the king of Mesene, Shapurdukhtag daughter of the king of Mesene, and Ohrmazddukhtag daughter of the king of the Sakas: for the soul [of each], one lamb, one *griw* of bread and 5 *hofan*, [and] 4 *pas* of wine;

## 39

and the lamb that is left over from there, so far as it lasts, likewise for those souls we have ordered to be worshipped.

## 40

And these are written who were under the rule of King Pabag: Sasan Orsigan, Farrag son of Farrag, Wahramad Horagan (?), Asporag son of Asporag, Puhrag Mardenagan, Zig the master of ceremonies (?), Shapur Wezanagan, Shapur Mihrbozanagan;

## 41

those who were under the rule of Ardashir king of kings: Sadaluf (?) king of Abrenag, Ardashir king of Merv, Ardashir king of Kerman, Ardashir king of the Sakas, Denag mother of King Pabag, Rodag mother of Ardashir king of kings,

## 42

Denag queen of queens, daughter of Pabag, Ardashir the *bidakhsh* (viceroy), Pabag the chiliarch, Dehen of the Waraz [family], Sasan of the Suren, Sasan lord of Andegan, Peroz of the Karin, Gog of the Karin, Abursam Ardashir-Farr, Gelman of Demavend, Rakhsh the commander of the army, Mard (?) the chief scribe, Pabag the master of ceremonies,

## 43

Pachihr Wisfarragan, Wifr Farragan, Mihrkhwasht Baresagan, Homfradad (?) the master of the Medes, Diran the master of arms, Chihrag the judge, Wardan the master of the stables, Mihrag Tosaragan, Zig Zabragan, Sagbus the master of the hunt, Hudug (?) the *grastbed*, Jahen the *madugdar* (?);

## 44

those who were under our rule: Ardashir king of Nodshiragan, Ardashir king of Kerman, Denag queen of Mesene, Shapur's own (?), Hamazasp king of Iberia, Prince Walakhsh, son of Pabag,

## 45

Prince Sasan, who was brought up among the Farragan, another Prince Sasan, who was brought up among the Kadugan, Prince Narseh son of Peroz, Prince Narseh son of Shapur, Shapur the *bidakhsh*, Pabag the chiliarch, Peroz the master of the horse, Ardashir of the Waraz [family],

## 46

Ardashir of the Suren, Narseh lord of Andegan, Ardashir of the Karin, Wohnam the minister, Frig satrap of Weh-Andiyok-Shapur, Sridoy (?) of the Shamust, Ardashir of Ardashir-Shnom, Pachihr of Tahm-Shapur, Ardashir satrap of Goyman (?), Chashmag of New-Shapur,

## 47

Wohnam of Shapur-Shnom, Tirmihr commander of the fortress of Shahrkerd, Zig the master of ceremonies, Ardawan of Demavend, Gundifarr Ewagan, Razmayod and Pabich (?) of Peroz-Shapur, of the Shanbidgan, Warzan (?) satrap of Gay, Kerdsraw the *bidakhsh*, Pabag Wisfarragan,

## 48

Walakhsh of Seleucia, Yazadbed the queen's counsellor, Pabag the sword-bearer, Narseh satrap of Rind, Tiyanag satrap of Hamadan, Wardbed the master of servants, Joymard Rastagan, Ardashir Wifragan (?), Abursam leader of Shapur's court, Narseh Barragan, Shapur Narsehgan,

## 49

Narseh the *grastbed*, Hormezd the chief scribe, Nadug the jailer, Pabag the master of the gate, Pasfard Pasfardgan, Abdakhsh of the fortress commanders, Kerdir the *herbed* (priest), Rastag satrap of Weh-Ardashir, Ardashir Bidakhshgan, Mihrkhwasht the treasurer, Shapur the minister,

## 50

Arshtad Mihran, scribe of the *frawardag*, Sasan of the harem, of the Sasanids, Wirod the market master (?), Ardashir satrap of Niriz, Baydad Wardbedagan, Kerdir son of Ardawan, Zurwandad Bandagan, Winnar of the Sasanids, Manzag (?) of the harem, Sasan the judge, Wardan Nashbedagan (?), Wardag the *warazbed* (?): for each one lamb, one *griw* of bread and 5 *hofan*, [and] 4 *pas* of wine.

## 51

Now, as we apply ourselves to the affairs and the works of the gods, and are the gods' own, and with the gods' help have sought out and hold all these lands, and have won fame and bravery, so too whoever comes after us and is fortunate, let him also apply himself to the affairs and the works of the gods, so that the gods may be his helpers and make him their own. This is the handwriting of me, Hormezd the scribe, son of Shilag the scribe.
